The default build links Lensfun and available static GLib dependencies into the executable. GLib is LGPL-2.1-or-later; gettext/libintl is LGPL-2.1-or-later; PCRE2 is BSD-3-Clause. The dependency versions and build paths come from pkg-config. Their source and licenses are provided by the corresponding development packages (https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/glib, https://www.gnu.org/software/gettext/, https://github.com/PCRE2Project/pcre2). The local executable is rebuilt and relinked through Cargo; these are build-time dependencies, not runtime Lensfun installation requirements.
